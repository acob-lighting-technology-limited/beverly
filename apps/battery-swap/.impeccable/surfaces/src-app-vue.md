# Surface brief: Beverly PUE (apps/battery-swap)

Mode: Operate. Staff run battery swaps, a rider and bike registry, live battery diagnostics and appliance sales across mini-grid sites. Pinned by the user: Apple liquid glass, the left rail stays, the Beverly Call yellow palette, and the same avatar dropdown as the rest of the Beverly platform (same auth).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Two materials with one job each. Glass for what floats (rail, tabs, menus, dialogs, tooltips); solid for what you read and fill in. The category default is a flat card mosaic; this refuses it by letting the floating chrome carry the glass and the content stay calm.

OWN-WORLD: Pearl and cream ground with slow drifting warm lights (yellow and a touch of peach). Glass has a bright specular rim, a soft inner glow and strong backdrop blur. Controls are pills; primary buttons are yellow gel with dark ink; inputs are inset glass. Light theme is frosted white glass over cream; dark is smoked glass over near-black.

STORY: A swap attendant sees where to act within a second and trusts the numbers: dashes for unknown readings, never zeros. Honest states stay honest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating glass rail left (icon beads, active bead glows yellow), a glass segmented tab bar with a sliding lens indicator, the avatar pill top right opening the shared Beverly account menu. Content sits on solid panels, not nested cards.

FORM: Pinned whole by the user in words: "Apple liquid glass", "keep the left side rail", seed: none (pinned).

SIGNATURE: The section tab lens slides between tabs with an exponential ease-out. Three selectable palettes (Beverly yellow, black and white, white and green) restyle every screen through tokens. Reduced motion turns the motion off.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
